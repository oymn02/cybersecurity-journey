## How I work

Every practical write-up aims to include:

1. **Objective** — the question or skill being explored.
2. **Environment and scope** — the lab or system used, with authorization clearly established.
3. **Method** — the steps and reasoning, not just the final result.
4. **Evidence** — relevant outputs, observations, or screenshots where appropriate.
5. **Impact and mitigations** — why the issue matters and how to reduce the risk.
6. **Lessons learned** — limitations, open questions, and next steps.

All offensive security exercises are restricted to intentionally vulnerable labs, systems I own, or environments where I have explicit permission to test.
